It has been a rough few months for the word of the year, habits. I was going
strong for the first few months, but the Australia trip really upended things.
There have been a few attempts at rallying but to no use. I have felt somewhat
defeated and downtrodden for the last few months. That is until a few weeks ago.
I saw a few clips circulating on YouTube about StreamerU and the one that really
struck me was Ludwig talking about succeeding at being a streamer. His advice
boiled down to write down a goal for the year as well as a 5 year goal. "Most
people over-estimate what they can do in one year, and under-estimate what they
can do in five," I think was the exact quote, I'm not bothering to look it up.
So here is what I have so far:

| Subject | 2026                                  | 2030                        |
| ------- | ------------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| Gamedev | 4 episodes/week of Handmade Hero      | Release something on Steam  |
| Guitar  | Memorize the fretboard                | Read sheet music            |
| Drums   | 100 BPM Singles, Doubles, Paradiddles | Play live with other people |
| Health  | Lose 20 lbs                           | Stay in shape               |
| Blog    | Post once a week                      | Develop a community         |
| TTRPG   | Run in person one shot                | Run an in person campaign   |
| Cycling | Overnight bike packing                | Complete the MS150 Century  |
| Drawing | Complete a drawing once a week        | Create assets for game      |

It's a lot. It's foolish. It's likely unattainable. But here I am writing my
weekly post, I have already lost 2 lbs, and I managed to do 3 episodes of HMH
this week. I have a lot more to do and will likely fail some of these, but it is
something to keep me busy and failing is one of my favorite things to do.
