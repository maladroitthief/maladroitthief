In tabletop news, I am making a pivot from hexmaps to grid. This makes it sound like I am some ambassador to hexagons or something. What is really driving this is the fact that I like to make hand drawn maps in my notebook and I can draw squares easier and faster than hexagons. This lead to some fun hacking of the [Chain Stocking the Hex Map](https://www.prismaticwasteland.com/blog/chain-stocking-the-hex-map) post from Prismatic Wasteland. I used this a few times and thought it was pretty cool, but with squares, I need to roll a d8 instead of a d6. This lead to me adding alignment and hybrid tiles. Alignment works like Good/Evil areas in Dwarf Fortress. Roll a d6 to get the actual tile and then roll another d6 to choose if it's good, evil, or savage. For hybrid tiles, roll 2d6 and get creative with the results. I have yet to actually play test this, but I am excited nonetheless.
